About Our School

A Home Away From Home for Every Child

Murliganj Public School is an established primary and upper primary school in Murliganj, Madhepura, offering structured education from Class 1 to Class 8 along with residential (hostel) facilities.

We focus on strong fundamentals, English communication, moral values and all-round development — helping children grow into confident, responsible citizens.

  • CBSE-based syllabus
  • Residential & day scholars
  • Experienced, caring teachers
  • Safe & disciplined campus
  • Regular tests & PTMs
  • Sports & co-curricular activities

Learning at Every Stage

A carefully planned curriculum that builds curiosity in young learners and strong concepts in older students.

Pre-Primary

Nursery · LKG · UKG

Class 1 – Class 5

Strong foundation in English, Hindi, Mathematics, EVS and Computer, with focus on reading and writing skills.

🔬

Upper Primary

Class 6 – Class 8

Science, Social Science, Mathematics and languages taught with practical examples, projects and regular assessment.
